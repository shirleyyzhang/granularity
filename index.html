I have $12k in a high-yield savings account, $6,500 on a credit card at 22% APR, and I just got a raise. Should I pay off the card first or start investing in my 401(k)?

Short answer: do both, but in a clear order. High-interest debt usually beats investing as a first priority — a guaranteed 22% return by paying the card off is hard for markets to match.

That said, don't skip an employer 401(k) match if you have one. That match is free money and often outweighs the interest you're paying on a smaller balance.

Before you throw every spare dollar at the card, keep a small emergency buffer so you don't re-borrow. Wiping savings to zero can push you back onto the card when something breaks.

Once the card is gone, use the raise to automate investing instead of lifestyle creep — that's how the raise compounds instead of disappearing into a nicer apartment.
